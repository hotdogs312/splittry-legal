Splittry legal / Account deletion

Delete your Splittry account

You can request deletion of your Splittry account and associated personal data by emailing htdogs312@gmail.com with the subject “Splittry account deletion”.

What to include

  • The email address used for your Splittry account.
  • Whether you want the whole account deleted or only specific data.
  • Enough information for us to verify that you own the account.

What we delete

  • Your account/profile record and login identity.
  • Your trip memberships and personal profile data.
  • Groups you own and their associated expenses, photos, settlements, wallet QR/images and activity records, where deletion is technically and legally permitted.

What may remain

Content you added to a group owned by someone else may remain visible to that group's members; we can remove your personal profile and membership. Backup copies and records required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations may be retained for a limited period. Expenses in a group you do not own may be retained so other members' records remain accurate.

Timing

We aim to respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before deleting data. Local-only data on your device can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage; deleting the app does not by itself delete an online account.

For privacy questions, see the Privacy Policy.
