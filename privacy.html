Privacy Policy

1. Scope

Splittry is an expense-sharing and settlement-recording app. It can be used in local-only mode or with an online account that syncs groups with invited trip members. This policy explains how data is handled when you use the app.

Splittry is not intended for children under 13. You must be at least 13 to use it.

2. Data we collect

Account data

  • Email address, display name and profile picture.
  • Account identifier from Supabase Auth.
  • If you use Google sign-in, the name, email and avatar returned by Google.

Trip and expense data

  • Group name, dates, currency, settlement settings and membership roles.
  • Expenses, amounts, currencies, categories, notes, splits and recorded settlements.
  • Activity-log entries showing who changed what inside a shared group.

Photos and receipts

Expense photos and receipt images you choose to attach. Receipt text may be processed on your device for OCR; it is not uploaded for OCR processing.

Payment-handoff data

Optional PayMe/Alipay links, IDs or receive-QR images you choose to share with members of your own trips. Splittry does not receive your bank credentials, card number or payment-provider password.

Premium passes

If you buy a one-time premium pass through Google Play, Google processes the payment. Splittry receives/verifies purchase information such as the product ID, purchase token and expiry so it can unlock the pass and record its duration. Splittry does not receive your full card details.

Technical data

Authentication/session data, IP address, device/browser metadata, timestamps and security/error logs needed to operate and protect the service. Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers and ad interaction data for free accounts as described below.

Local data

Local-mode groups, expenses, app settings and cached account data are stored on your device.

3. Why we use data

  • Create/authenticate accounts and secure access.
  • Provide, sync and back up groups and expenses.
  • Show shared trip information to members of the same trip.
  • Display wallet links/QR codes only to the relevant trip participants.
  • Verify and manage one-time premium passes.
  • Prevent abuse, troubleshoot and comply with law.

4. Sharing

  • Trip members: people in the same online group can see its members, expenses, balances, settlement records, activity log and wallet links/QRs relevant to that group. Only join groups you trust.
  • Supabase: authentication, database, real-time and private file storage are hosted by Supabase as a data processor.
  • Google: Google sign-in and Google Play purchases are processed by Google. Free accounts show Google AdMob ads; Google may process device/advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data under Google's policies.
  • Payment providers: opening PayMe/Alipay or showing a QR hands off to that provider. Splittry does not process or confirm the payment.
  • We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with third-party advertisers beyond the Google AdMob processing described here.

5. Security

Splittry uses server-side Row Level Security, private storage buckets and short-lived signed URLs so data is limited to authenticated trip members. The public Supabase anon key is safe to ship; the service-role key is never shipped in the app. No system is completely secure; do not put sensitive credentials in expense notes or photos.

6. Retention and deletion

Account and trip data is retained while needed to provide the service and meet legal/security obligations. Local data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. Online trip data remains visible to other members until deleted or the account is deleted.

To request account deletion, follow the instructions on the account deletion page. We may need to verify your identity. Backup/legal retention may delay final removal.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, or object to processing. Contact the email above. We may need to verify your identity.

8. International transfers

Supabase and Google may process data outside Hong Kong. We use providers with contractual and security measures appropriate to the service.

9. Premium passes and payments

Premium passes are one-time purchases sold through Google Play. They do not auto-renew. Google Play's payment, refund and tax terms apply. Splittry is not a bank, payment processor, money transmitter or financial adviser and does not execute expense settlements.

10. Children

Splittry is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date above. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies.
